## Conduct

- One owner per franchise. Owners manage their own lineups and transactions.
- The commissioner can act for any franchise on MFL when needed (league administration). Coach's owner credential cannot.
- Collusion and roster dumping are not allowed; the commissioner reviews suspicious trades (see trades-and-waivers.md).
